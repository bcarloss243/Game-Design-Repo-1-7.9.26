# Halcyon Academy · First Weather — v0.3

A two-day playable Unity narrative prototype directed by Bergen Carloss. This revision stays in Unity and GitHub. No new executable or ZIP is produced until Bergen approves this phase.

## Play in Unity

Open this project with Unity 6000.3.14f1. Choose **Halcyon → 1 Open First Weather**, then press Play. Begin a new story from the title, or continue the existing save. Development previews disable saving; stop and restart Play mode to return to normal play.

Visit the residence, attend the first class, and choose up to two different afternoon activities. Studying prepares the assignment; rest and company change the next morning. Return home for the evening: call Alaric, message Jules, write in the journal, or sleep. The second class responds to preparation and Pressure. Find Lola in the greenhouse, allocate scarce water twice, then read her first message to complete the slice.

The garden has six measures and cannot meet every demand. Keep each bed alive while honoring the chosen mentor’s priority: the kitchen harvest for Marchand, young roots for Thibodaux. Ask Lola for qualitative clues. Garden Restored and Work in Progress are both complete endings; leaving a note finishes an unfinished garden. There is no timed response or game-over punishment for a classroom episode.

## Controls and access

Click choices; Tab/Shift+Tab move through buttons and Enter activates them. Space advances a page without choices. J opens the journal, M toggles audio, Esc pauses/resumes. Journal typing captures letter shortcuts. Drag/scroll explores the map and academy; Command/Ctrl + scroll zooms, with visible zoom/recenter controls as alternatives. Academy also supports arrows, +/− and Home. Larger story text and reduced motion persist in Settings. All clues have written equivalents. Content note: family pressure and anxiety/panic described in text.

Progress saves locally after choices, event changes, and journal editing. One save slot; New Game replaces it when selected from the title. Version-one saves are backed up locally before migration. Progress made in developer previews is deliberately not saved.

## September revision

The painted September title supplies the visual direction: Cinzel Decorative headings, Cormorant body type, indigo painted panels, warm gold rules and portrait-led presentation. New portraits, residence, classroom, greenhouse, district and coherent courtyard artwork replace the former style. Full-body painted student poses replace geometric people. The academy has a live clock, student routes, window light, water reflections and a near foreground layer. Other rooms have restrained lighting/mote/reflection ambience. This is layered 2D art, not a fully modeled 3D environment.

Pressure now changes prose, sound, stability and classroom consequences. Three morning choices support Vapeur, sitting with the hum, or leaving. Vapeur helps and imposes a floor; repeated use stabilizes pressure, skipping after use has rebound, and sitting builds tolerance. Hidden morning variance and yesterday’s actions persist. A severe episode costs the remaining field seminar and produces a recovery timetable. Lola’s presence affects the gauge without a relationship meter. The evening, typed journal, family messages, Rootwork clues, mentor-dependent garden and final dispatch make the two-day loop visible.

## Verification and limits

Current automated evidence lives in V03-Rules.txt, V03-Screens.txt, V03-Playthrough.txt, V03-Fallback-Playthrough.txt, AcademyVerification.txt and MapInterfaceVerification.txt. Reports include their own timestamps. Earlier Verification.txt and UI reports describe older versions. Automated runs do not replace the three human playtests required by the assignment.

The slice does not implement the full quarter calendar, all Ward travel, dependency/endgame arcs, or late revelations. Seeded material is listed in REVISION-CHECKLIST.md. The student atlas contains two repeating painted designs and simple four-frame poses; it is not a finished crowd animation library. Room ambience is subtle and the audio is synthesized prototype material. The title-reference publication provenance must be confirmed before distribution. The human feedback, reflection and video materials have explicit completion requirements in their respective documents.

Do not use the existing Build Mac Prototype menu until packaging has been approved. The repository’s earlier downloadable build does not represent this revision.
