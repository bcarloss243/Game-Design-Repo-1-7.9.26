# Art provenance

All five image files were copied without image manipulation into `Resources/HalcyonArt` and imported as Unity textures. The UI crops them at runtime to fit the screen. New art was generated on September 8, 2026 with OpenAI image generation.

| Asset | Source and use |
| --- | --- |
| cover.png | User-supplied “Screenshot 2026-09-08 at 11.28.24 AM.png”, Halcyon Academy with Lola and Molly. Title screen and main reference. Original creator/license not supplied. |
| map.png | Newly generated illustrated Halcyon map: Mississippi crescent, balconies, Academy, greenhouses and the Cloche. |

| academy.png | Newly generated Academy lecture theater, brass climate model and students. |
| greenhouse.png | Newly generated Molly and Lola scene, with character appearance guided by the supplied reference. |

The three September 7 screenshots guided the world direction: retrofuturist canal streets, raised glass growing houses, and Ninth Ward homes and walkways. They are not bundled into the game. Generated illustrations are prototype interpretations; exact geography, architecture and costume details remain subject to creative review.

Art direction: detailed ink contour and crosshatching, softly painted skin, olive uniforms, aged brass, mint water and lilac storm light. The output follows the supplied visual reference without naming an artist as its source.

Sound is synthesized from sine tones and filtered noise within the runtime. It contains no downloaded recordings, samples or copyrighted songs. The music is a quiet repeating sketch, suitable for the prototype rather than a final soundtrack.
