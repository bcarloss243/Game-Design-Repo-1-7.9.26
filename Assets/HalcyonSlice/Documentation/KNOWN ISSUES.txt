KNOWN ISSUES AND LIMITATIONS — FIRST WEATHER v0.3.0

No game-breaking defect was observed in the complete standalone playthrough
on the development Mac. This is limited test evidence, not a guarantee.

- Mac only, macOS 12+. Apple M3 execution tested. Intel binaries are included,
  but execution on Intel and first launch on a second Mac remain untested.
- Not Apple notarized. A downloaded copy may need the per-app Open Anyway
  approval described in START HERE.txt. A managed school Mac may prevent it.
- Retina displays may open a small initial window. Choose Window > Fill,
  resize the window, or use the green full-screen control for easier reading.
- The layout uses a 16:9 safe area; other shapes may have letterboxing.
- One local save. Starting a new story replaces it. Saves from older releases
  or the Unity editor may use a different app identity.
- Switching apps pauses automatically. Return to the game and press Esc.
- The world uses layered 2D paintings and ambient animation. It is not a
  freely rotatable 3D city. Two student designs and simple poses repeat.
- The two-day slice is complete. Wider Ward travel, the full quarter,
  long-term project systems and later story arcs are outside this prototype.
- Story text and synthesized audio remain prototype material.
- Native trackpad comfort and broader display/accessibility coverage still
  benefit from human testing; engineering checks do not establish comfort.
- Unity logged thread-finalization and ComputeBuffer disposal warnings at
  shutdown. No crash, lost save, or blocked progression accompanied them in
  this test. The build also reported a Unity Services symbol-upload warning;
  cloud debug-symbol upload was unavailable, but the app built and ran.

If progress seems unclear, open How to play or check the map’s timetable.
Both Garden Restored and Work in Progress finish the game. Garden hints and
leaving a note are supported options, with no timed input requirement.
