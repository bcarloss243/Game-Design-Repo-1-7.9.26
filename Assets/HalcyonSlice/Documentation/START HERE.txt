HALCYON ACADEMY: FIRST WEATHER
Version 0.3.0 | September 24, 2026 | Bergen Carloss

PLAY
1. Extract the ZIP. Keep the application intact.
2. Open “Halcyon Academy - First Weather.app”. Unity is not required.
3. Choose “Begin a new story”. Begin at the Residence on the map.
4. If the window is small, choose Window > Fill in the Mac menu bar.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 12 or later. The app includes Apple Silicon and Intel executables.
Tested on an Apple M3 Mac; Intel execution has not been tested.
This download does not run on Windows, Android, or in a browser.

FIRST LAUNCH
This educational prototype is locally signed, but is not Apple notarized.
macOS may block an app downloaded from the internet. If you trust this
GitHub release, first try opening the app, then go to System Settings >
Privacy & Security > Open Anyway and confirm the app you intended to open.
Apple’s instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-au/102445
Do not disable macOS security protections globally.

CONTROLS
Click a choice. Tab / Shift+Tab selects buttons; Enter activates them.
Space turns a story page without choices. Esc pauses or resumes.
J opens the journal. M toggles audio. Letter shortcuts stop while typing.
Drag or scroll to explore the map and academy. Command + scroll zooms;
visible zoom and recenter buttons are also available.
Settings offers larger story text and reduced motion.
Switching to another app automatically pauses the story; Esc resumes it.

THE TWO-DAY STORY
Attend class, choose a mentor, spend two afternoon activity slots, return
home, and decide how to spend the evening. On day two, attend the assessment
and find Lola in the greenhouse. Allocate six measures of water among three
beds, then reconsider after the shade moves. Ask Lola for clues.

Continue through Lola’s final message. “Complete · Garden Restored” and
“Complete · Work in Progress” are both valid endings. Leaving a garden note
lets you finish without solving it. There are no timed choices.
Allow roughly 10–15 minutes for a first visit; reading speed and exploration
vary. The representative route contains about 6–8 minutes of reading alone.

SAVING AND QUITTING
One automatic local save. Continue resumes it; Begin a new story replaces it.
Use Pause > Quit, or Quit on the title screen, to leave safely.
The download contains no test save. Saves belong to the Mac user account.
Older prototype/editor saves may use a different app identity and may not
appear in this version’s Continue option.

Content note: family pressure and anxiety/panic described in text.
Read Documentation/KNOWN ISSUES.txt for platform and prototype limitations.
Credits and complete license notices are included and accessible in-game.
